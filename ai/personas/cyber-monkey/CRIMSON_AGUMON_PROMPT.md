## 📐 **Technical Specs for Terminal Background:**

**Image Requirements:**
- **Resolution:** 2560x1440 or 1920x1080
- **Format:** JPG (Warp supports .jpg, .jpeg, .JPEG)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Positioning:** Character on RIGHT 30% of frame, LEFT 70% empty/dark
- **Background Color:** #0E0E0F (very dark, almost black)
- **Color Profile:** sRGB

**Composition Guidelines:**
- Character sits/stands on right side
- Faces LEFT (toward the terminal text area)
- Dark enough background to not distract
- Crimson accents visible but subtle
- Lots of negative space for readability

## 🚀 **Update Warp Theme:**

Once you have the image, update the theme file:

```yaml
background_image:
  path: crimson_agumon.jpg
  opacity: 20
```

Adjust opacity:
- **10-15:** Very subtle, barely visible
- **20-25:** Noticeable but not distracting (RECOMMENDED)
- **30-40:** More prominent
- **50+:** Too strong for terminal use
